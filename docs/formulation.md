# Mathematical formulation work

Josh leads this deliverable, working with Dora on data and Izzy on implementation.
The full mathematical formulation has not been written or approved yet.

The proposal calls for minimum-cost weekly assignment of all required repairs,
each exactly once, to qualified contractors within their available hours. Define
sets, parameters, decision variables, units, objective, and each constraint with
its business interpretation. Explicitly document whether repair durations depend
on the contractor and how costs are measured; these details are not specified
by the proposal and must be agreed rather than invented.

Keep exact appointment times and travel routing outside the current scope.
Overtime and repair priority are unresolved proposal comments, not adopted model
features. Do not add a workload-balancing objective based on the proposal filename;
the written objective is total cost.

Completion requires a readable formulation, justified assumptions, an independently
checked small instance, and a review of its correspondence with Python code.
